Bio

With no superpowers but unmatched precision, Clint Barton—aka Hawkeye—proves that courage isn’t cosmic. A master archer and skilled field agent, Clint brings humanity and sharp wit to the Avengers. Whether fighting gods or standing up for his family, he always hits the mark. He's the team’s quiet anchor—loyal, grounded, and lethal from 500 yards away.
