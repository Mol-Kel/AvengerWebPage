Black Widow – Natasha Romanoff

Alias: Master Spy and Assassin

Affiliation: Avengers/S.H.I.E.L.D

Weapon of Choice: Martial Arts, Espionage, Twin Batons

First Appearance (MCU): Iron Man 2 (2010)

Bio

Trained in the infamous Red Room, Natasha Romanoff walked a long path from spy to superhero. As Black Widow, she brought deadly precision, tactical genius, and emotional depth to the Avengers. A master of espionage and combat, Natasha used her skills not just to fight—but to atone. Her bravery and sacrifice cemented her legacy as a hero who saved lives not with powers, but with purpose.
