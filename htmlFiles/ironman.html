Bio

Genius. Billionaire. Philanthropist. Hero. Tony Stark redefined heroism on his own terms when he built the first Iron Man suit in a cave. Ever-evolving in both technology and character, Tony became the brains behind the Avengers, developing game-changing tech and making the ultimate sacrifice to save the universe. With unmatched wit and a suit for every scenario, Iron Man is a legend who proved that courage is the best armor.
