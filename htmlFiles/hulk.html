First Appearance (MCU): The Incredible Hulk (2008)

Bio

Dr. Bruce Banner is a brilliant scientist cursed (or blessed) with a monstrous alter ego—the Hulk. Fueled by gamma radiation, the Hulk is an unstoppable force of nature, capable of immense destruction and unlikely compassion. As Banner struggles for control, he discovers that true strength lies in balance. Whether smashing enemies or solving equations, he remains a vital part of the Avengers’ strength and soul.
