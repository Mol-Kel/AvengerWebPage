Bio

Frozen in ice and awakened in a new era, Steve Rogers is the heart and soul of the Avengers. Enhanced by the Super Soldier Serum during WWII, Steve became Captain America, a symbol of justice, sacrifice, and unshakable moral compass. With his iconic shield and battlefield tactics, Cap led the Avengers through their darkest hours. Whether standing alone or leading an army, he always fights “for the little guy.”
